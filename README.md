**Neon Bay Nights** — a top-down open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built as an original game with its own city, characters, story and art. Explore Neon Bay on foot and by car: a driveable road-grid city with six districts (Old Town, Uptown, Downtown, Marina, Harbor, Suburbs), traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted-star system with patrol cars and foot cops who chase you down, combat, a five-job story chain from Mara at the Marina Club, taxi fares and cash stashes, a HUD with a minimap, health, money and wanted display, a day/night cycle, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, and a save that keeps your money and job progress between visits.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, just static files under `game/`.
